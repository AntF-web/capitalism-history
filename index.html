Capitalism develops through linked institutions: trade, state power, private property, credit, forced labor, wage labor, machinery, corporations, and financial markets.

System layers

Capitalism was built through overlapping layers. Each layer has its own institutions, conflicts, and historical violence.

⚖️

Property

Land, labor, ideas, and resources are turned into ownable and sellable assets.

🛳️

Trade

Long-distance trade links local production to global markets and colonial routes.

🏦

Banking

Credit, deposits, public debt, and central banks make accumulation scalable.

⛓️

Slavery

Forced labor, plantation economies, and racial domination generate enormous wealth.

🏭

Industry

Factories reorganize time, labor, machines, cities, and discipline around production.

📈

Finance

Stocks, bonds, speculation, derivatives, debt, and crises become engines of power.

Note: This timeline is interpretive and critical. It does not claim that capitalism began at one exact moment. It shows major institutions and historical processes that helped build the capitalist world-system.
